# breathEN — SEO Plan (the short version)

The goal is simple: **rank for these queries.**

---

## 1. The Keywords

1. **Portable oxygen can**
2. **99% oxygen can**
3. **Oxygen for high altitude**
4. **Travel oxygen can**
5. **Oxy99** — *see note below*

---

## 2. One Honest Note About "Oxy99"

Oxy99 is already a real brand with its own website (Zeneris Pharma). Google will always show *their* site first for the exact word "Oxy99" — nobody can outrank a brand for its own name.

But most people searching "Oxy99" don't mean the brand, they mean *any* 99% oxygen can. So we capture those buyers two ways:

- **Rank for "99% oxygen can"** — the generic term people actually search
- **Make a "breathEN vs Oxy99" page** — ranks for "Oxy99 alternative", "best oxygen can India" etc., and puts you next to them when people compare

---

## 3. What We'll Actually Do

**Step 1 — Make the site rankable (one-time setup)**
Sitemap, robots.txt, structured data (so Google can show product/FAQ rich results), proper titles/descriptions, Google Search Console + Analytics connected.

**Step 2 — Build one page per keyword**
- `/portable-oxygen-can/`
- `/99-oxygen-can/`
- `/oxygen-for-high-altitude/`
- `/travel-oxygen-can/`
- `/breathen-vs-oxy99/`

Each page optimised properly: keyword in the title, heading, first paragraph, URL, and linked from the homepage.

**Step 3 — Supporting articles (the blogs)**
This is what actually makes the pages rank. Each cluster gets articles that all link back to its keyword page:

- **High altitude:** Kedarnath oxygen guide, Ladakh checklist, altitude sickness symptoms, oxygen can vs cylinder for trekking
- **Travel:** oxygen cans on flights, road trips, trains
- **Purity:** what 99% oxygen means, is recreational oxygen safe
- **Comparison:** breathEN vs Oxy99, best portable oxygen can India

Start with the High Altitude cluster (best buying intent), then the rest — 2–4 articles a month.

**Step 4 — Keep improving**
Monthly check: what's ranking, what moved, what needs a push. Re-optimise the pages that are close to page 1 (the fastest wins), and keep building the clusters.
