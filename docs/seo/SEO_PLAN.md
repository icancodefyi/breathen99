## 3. Honest Position on "Oxy99"

**Oxy99 is an existing, well-established competitor brand** (Zeneris Pharma; own domains oxy99.co.in / oxy99.in; sold on 1mg, Flipkart, IndiaMART). Google will always show Oxy99's own websites first for the exact word "Oxy99". It is **not realistically winnable** as a keyword, and directly targeting a competitor's brand name also carries trademark risk.

**However**, many Indians type "Oxy99" loosely to mean *any* 99% oxygen can. This gives us two winnable opportunities:

1. **Own the generic term "99% oxygen can"** — captures the same searches, fully winnable.
2. **Build a "breathEN vs Oxy99" comparison page** — ranks for "Oxy99 alternative", "Oxy99 vs breathEN", "best oxygen can like Oxy99", and converts shoppers comparing brands.

> **Client decision needed:** We recommend *not* targeting the exact "Oxy99" word. We target the generic term + comparison page instead. Please confirm.

## 5. Technical SEO Foundation (Quick Wins)

Delivered in the first sprint so every page we publish is indexable:

1. **`sitemap.xml`** — auto-generated listing all pages
2. **`robots.txt`** — allow crawl, reference sitemap
3. **Canonical tags** — prevent duplicate-content issues
4. **Unique meta titles & descriptions** per page, keyword-matched
5. **Structured data (JSON-LD)** — Product, Organization, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList → enables rich results (star ratings, FAQs in search)
6. **Open Graph + Twitter Cards** — proper social share previews with absolute image URLs
7. **Optimised OG image** (~1200×630) branded for social shares
8. **`noindex` thankyou page** — prevent payment pages showing in Google
9. **Analytics + Google Search Console** — measure everything, submit sitemap
