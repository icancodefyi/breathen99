## 3. Validation Criteria (Keyword Sweet Spot)

For each keyword, score 1–5 on:

- **Demand** — are people actually searching this? (validate with Ubersuggest/Surfer/GSC)
- **Fit** — does it sit in our funnel and map to a page we'll build?
- **Intent** — can we satisfy what the searcher wants? (check what currently ranks — that's the intent)
- **Difficulty** — can a new-ish site realistically rank? (target Low–Med; avoid Med-High until authority grows)

**Rule:** transactional keywords → money pages. Informational keywords → supporting content that links back to money pages. A keyword without a conversion path is wasted traffic.

## 5. On-Page Rules Applied to Every Page (Three Kings)

For each target keyword:

1. **Title tag** — front-load keyword, <60 chars, compelling. _(Huntress-style: keyword-rich + benefit driven.)_
2. **H1** — exactly 1, mirrors the target keyword.
3. **First sentence/paragraph** — keyword in the opening body copy, answer-style (not flowery intro).
4. **URL** — `/target-keyword/` short + descriptive.
5. **Meta description** — 150–160 chars, keyword + CTA, written like ad copy.
6. **H2/H3s** — weave cluster keywords naturally into subheadings.
7. **Internal links** — every page links to its cluster money page + related pages (3–5 links minimum).
8. **Images** — descriptive alt text with keywords where natural, WebP, compressed.

## 6. AI-Era Additions (from `/seo-skills/`)

- **Comparison ecosystem:** `/breathen-vs-oxy99/`, `/oxygen-can-vs-cylinder/`, `/breathen-vs-boost-oxygen/` → LLM brand-mention cheat code. Also publish "best portable oxygen can in India" buyers guide that includes competitors — makes breathEN the category authority.
- **Category reframing:** breathEN ≠ "an oxygen can". Own the lane: **"India's nose-breathing oxygen canister"** / **"the recreational oxygen brand that follows your natural breathing pathway"**. Gives ChatGPT a distinct recommendation slot vs Oxy99 (Chili Piper/Help Scout model).
- **Third-party reviews:** push for reviews on 1mg/Amazon/Google Business Profile — LLMs use review density as trust signal. (Client action item.)
- **Fan-out coverage:** for every target keyword, cover the sub-questions (People Also Ask, related searches) — sites are 161% more likely to be cited in AI Overviews when they also rank the fan-out queries.
- **Keep traditional SEO priority:** all of the above only works because it's built on pages that rank in Google/Bing first.
