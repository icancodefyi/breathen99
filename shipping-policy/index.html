Shipping Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Where we deliver

We ship to addresses across India. Delivery to some remote pin codes may take longer or may not be possible. If we cannot deliver to your address, we will contact you and refund your payment in full.

2. Shipping charges

Shipping is free on all orders within India.

3. Timelines

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 business days of your payment being confirmed.
  • Delivery: typically 5–7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.

These are estimates. Delays can happen because of weather, courier issues, holidays or other events outside our control.

4. Tracking

When your order ships we will share the courier and tracking details by email or phone using the details you gave at checkout. Please make sure your phone number, email and address are correct.

5. Failed deliveries

If the courier cannot reach you after several attempts, the parcel may be returned to us. We will contact you to arrange re-delivery, which may involve extra shipping charges, or a refund where appropriate.

6. Damaged on arrival

Please check your parcel on delivery. If it is visibly damaged, take photos and follow our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
