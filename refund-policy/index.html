Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

We want you to be happy with your order. Because breathEN is a sealed, pressurised product, our return rules are different from ordinary goods. Please read this policy before you buy.

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel before your order is dispatched. Email info@breathen99.com or call +91 98192 50202 with your order details.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled, but you may still use the returns process below if it qualifies.
  • If we cancel your order, you get a full refund.

2. Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your canister arrives damaged, defective or is not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery at info@breathen99.com. Please include your order details and clear photos or a short video of the item and packaging. After we verify the issue we will send a replacement or refund you in full.

3. What we cannot take back

  • Canisters or masks that have been opened, used or tampered with, for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • Items damaged after delivery through misuse, or stored or handled against the instructions.
  • Requests made after the reporting window above, unless the law requires otherwise.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • Allow about 5–7 business days for the money to reach your account after we approve the refund. Timing depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • If a payment is deducted but the order fails, the amount is normally returned automatically by Razorpay. If it is not, contact us with the payment reference.
