Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how breathEN (“breathEN”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses your personal data when you browse our website or place an order. We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

1. Data we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, the items and quantity you order, and any coupon code you use.
  • Payment status: your payment is processed by Razorpay. We receive a payment reference and the payment status, but we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used to keep the site secure and working.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

2. Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order, including sending order updates.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep our website and checkout secure.
  • To meet accounting, tax and other legal obligations.
  • To respond to your questions and complaints.

We rely on your consent and on the need to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Razorpay for payment processing.
  • Courier and logistics partners for delivery (name, phone, address).
  • Hosting and database providers (for example Vercel and MongoDB Atlas) that store and serve our website and orders.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Our website fonts are hosted on our own servers, so your browser does not contact Google to load them. Some of the providers above may process data outside India.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order data for as long as needed to fulfil your order, handle returns and disputes, and meet accounting and legal requirements. After that we delete or anonymise it.

5. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restrict access to order data, validate all inputs on our servers, and rely on PCI-compliant payment processing. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update inaccurate data;
  • erase your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent for future processing.

To do any of this, email info@breathen99.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Grievances

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, contact our grievance officer, at info@breathen99.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
